New Grad RN Resident – Cardiovascular ICU — Mercy Hospital

Cleveland Clinic · Canton, Ohio · Verified 25 November 2026

A new graduate RN residency in the 12-bed Cardiovascular ICU at Cleveland Clinic Mercy Hospital in Canton, Ohio, caring for patients after open-heart surgery. The schedule rotates days and nights, 7:00 AM to 7:30 PM or 7:00 PM to 7:30 AM, and the pay range is $35.50 to $55.60 per hour. It is a one-year accredited Nurse Residency Program with nine tracks, open to new graduate RNs and nurses with under a year of experience. The posting asks for an associate or bachelor’s degree in nursing, an Ohio RN license and a Basic Life Support certificate, and it mentions RN loan repayment, tuition reimbursement and relocation assistance.
