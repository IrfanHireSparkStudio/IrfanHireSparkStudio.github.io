Registered Nurse – Family Birth Center — Mayo Clinic

Mayo Clinic · Austin, Minnesota · Verified 25 November 2026

A part-time registered nurse role — 48 hours per pay period — in the Family Birth Center at Mayo Clinic in Austin, Minnesota, paying $40.19 to $61.18 per hour. Shifts are 12-hour nights with every third weekend and a holiday rotation. Newly graduated nurses with less than 12 months of practice are automatically enrolled in Mayo’s Nurse Residency Program, and one year of RN experience is preferred but not required. The posting asks for a Minnesota RN license and a Basic Life Support certificate.
