New Grad RN Residency — LewisGale Hospital Montgomery

HCA Healthcare · Blacksburg, Virginia · Verified 25 November 2026

A year-long paid residency for new graduate nurses at LewisGale Hospital Montgomery in Blacksburg, Virginia, hiring for 2027 residencies with an estimated hourly wage of $29.39 to $44.09. Tracks include the Emergency Department, ICU, Cardiac Medical Unit, Med Surg Orthopedics and the Operating Room. No previous experience is needed — the posting asks for an associate or bachelor’s degree in nursing, an RN license or graduate nurse status, Basic Life Support within 30 days and Advanced Cardiac Life Support within one year.
