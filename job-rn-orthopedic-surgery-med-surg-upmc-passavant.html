Registered Nurse (RN) – Orthopedic Surgery & Med Surg — UPMC Passavant

UPMC · Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania · Verified 25 November 2026

A full-time registered nurse role on 5 Pavilion, a 30-bed orthopedic surgery and medical-surgical unit at UPMC Passavant in Pittsburgh. The posting welcomes both new graduates and experienced nurses. Eligible graduates can receive a $20,000 graduate nurse loan repayment, plus up to $6,000 a year in tuition reimbursement and 5.5 weeks of paid time off. It asks for a Pennsylvania RN license, an associate or bachelor’s degree in nursing and a BLS or CPR certificate.
