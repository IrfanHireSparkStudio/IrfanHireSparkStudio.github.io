New Grad RN Residency — HCA HealthONE Aurora

HCA Healthcare · Aurora, Colorado · Verified live on the official posting: 1 November 2026

A year-long paid residency program for new graduate nurses at HCA HealthONE Aurora. The program is built for the move from student nurse to registered nurse, with hands-on clinical training, monthly education sessions, preceptorship, skills simulations, mentoring, and an evidence-based practice project.

What the official posting lists

  • Hourly wage estimate: $36.68 – $53.52 per hour
  • Cohort start dates include 9 November, 23 November, 7 December and 21 December 2026, with further cohorts into 2027
  • Units include Neuro Trauma, Med/Surg Oncology, Cardiac Care, Adult Psych and Rehab
  • No previous experience needed
  • Associate or Bachelor's degree in Nursing from an accredited program; RN license or Graduate Nurse status
  • Basic Cardiac Life Support (BLS) within 30 days of employment; Advanced Cardiac Life Support (ACLS) within 1 year
