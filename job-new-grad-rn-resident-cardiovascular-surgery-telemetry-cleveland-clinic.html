New Grad RN Resident – Cardiovascular Surgery & Telemetry — Cleveland Clinic

Cleveland Clinic · Cleveland, Ohio · Verified 25 November 2026

A new graduate RN residency on J61, a 24-bed cardiovascular telemetry unit at Cleveland Clinic in Cleveland, caring for patients after open-heart surgery and those with ventricular assist devices. The schedule rotates days and nights, 7:00 AM to 7:30 PM or 7:00 PM to 7:30 AM, with weekend and holiday rotation, and the pay range is $35.50 to $55.60 per hour. It is open to new graduate RNs and nurses with under a year of experience, and it asks for an associate or bachelor’s degree in nursing, an Ohio RN license and a Basic Life Support certificate. The residency includes a core curriculum and a success coach.
