New Grad RN Nurse Residency Cardiac Procedural — Catholic Medical Center

HCA Healthcare · Manchester, New Hampshire · Verified 25 November 2026

A year-long paid residency for new graduate nurses in the cardiac procedural area at Catholic Medical Center in Manchester, New Hampshire — a 330-bed acute care hospital that hosts the New England Heart & Vascular Institute and is a Level III Trauma Center. Start dates are flexible, and no previous experience is needed. The posting asks for an associate or bachelor’s degree in nursing, an RN license or graduate nurse status, Basic Life Support within 30 days and Advanced Cardiac Life Support within one year. Pay is not listed on the official posting.
