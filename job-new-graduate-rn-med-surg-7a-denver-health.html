New Graduate Registered Nurse (RN) – Med/Surg 7A — Denver Health

Denver Health · Denver, Colorado · Verified 25 November 2026

A new graduate registered nurse role on 7A, a 28-bed medical/surgical telemetry unit at Denver Health in Denver, Colorado, caring for cardiac patients with a 1:5 nurse-to-patient ratio. The job is full-time at 0.9 FTE on night shifts, paying $36.00 to $57.77 per hour, and it includes a 12-month Nurse Residency Program with monthly education classes. It is offered to nurses with no previous RN experience, and it asks for an associate degree in nursing, a Colorado RN license and a Basic Life Support certificate.
