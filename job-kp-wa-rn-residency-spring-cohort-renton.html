KP WA RN Residency Program, Spring Cohort — Kaiser Permanente

Kaiser Permanente · Renton, Washington · Verified 25 November 2026

A full-time, day-shift RN residency for the Spring cohort at Kaiser Permanente Washington in Renton, paying $34.81 to $53.18 per hour. Hiring rolls from February to April 2027, and the residency is open to new graduates and RNs with less than six months of experience. Placements cover Med/Surg, the Emergency Department and ambulatory clinics, where there are no weekends or holidays. The posting asks for an associate degree in nursing, a Washington RN license and a Basic Life Support certificate.
