National Resident RN ICU — Saint Joseph London

CommonSpirit Health · London, Kentucky · Verified 25 November 2026

A National Resident RN role in the Medical ICU at Saint Joseph London in London, Kentucky, paying $30.00 per hour on full-time nights, 7:00 PM to 7:00 AM. This is for the February 2027 cohort only, with a sign-on bonus of up to $10,000 and up to $24,000 in student loan assistance. It is open to nurses with an associate degree and zero to 12 months of RN experience. The posting asks for a Kentucky RN license and Basic Life Support on hire, with ACLS within 90 days and TNCC within 12 months; a bachelor’s degree is preferred.
