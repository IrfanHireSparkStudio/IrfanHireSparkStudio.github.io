New Grad RN Residency Emergency Department — TriCities Hospital

HCA Healthcare · Hopewell, Virginia · Verified 25 November 2026

A year-long paid residency for new graduate nurses in the Emergency Department at TriCities Hospital in Hopewell, Virginia, with an estimated hourly wage of $33.74 to $50.62. The department is accredited for stroke and chest pain care, and start dates are flexible, with shifts depending on unit need. No previous experience is needed — the posting asks for an associate or bachelor’s degree in nursing, an RN license or graduate nurse status, Basic Life Support within 30 days and Advanced Cardiac Life Support within one year.
