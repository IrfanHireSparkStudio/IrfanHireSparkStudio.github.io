Clinical Nurse RN – Medical Neuroscience Unit — Sharp Memorial Hospital

Sharp HealthCare · San Diego, California · Verified 25 November 2026

A full-time night-shift clinical nurse role in the Medical Neuroscience Unit at Sharp Memorial Hospital in San Diego, paying $64.21 to $93.76 per hour. Shifts run 7:00 PM to 7:30 AM, 12 hours, every other weekend. Registered nurses with less than 12 months of experience complete the new graduate requirements, so this is a genuine route in for new grads. The posting asks for a California RN license, an American Heart Association BLS certificate and an NIH Stroke Scale certificate.
